Support

Need help? Email supportvictoribus@gmail.com — we read every message and reply within a few business days.

Common questions

“I can't sign in.”
Make sure you're connected to the internet, then try again. If you signed in with Apple, check that you haven't revoked access in Settings → Apple ID → Sign in with Apple.

“My rating didn't update.”
A match's rating only applies once everyone confirms the result, or 24 hours pass without a dispute. Check the match status under “My matches.”

“I'm not getting notifications.”
Check Settings → Notifications on your device, and Settings → Notifications in the app. Notifications for invites, results, and referee duties are delivered even when the app is closed.

“How do I delete my account?”
Settings → Account → Delete Account. This removes your profile, match history, and any data we hold about you.

“How do I leave a leaderboard?”
Settings → Privacy → Local leaderboard → Off. You disappear immediately.

Reporting a bug

Email supportvictoribus@gmail.com with:

  • Your handle
  • What you were doing
  • What you expected to happen
  • What actually happened
  • Your device and iOS version (Settings → General → About)

Reporting abuse

Use the in-app Report button on a user's profile, or email supportvictoribus@gmail.com with the offending user's handle and what they did. You can also Block any user from their profile. We review reports and remove content or eject offenders, typically within 24 hours.
