Last updated 14 September 2026

Forum Victorum is built privacy-first. This page explains exactly what we collect and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Account info you give us: handle, display name, optional avatar, optional bio and home city. If you sign in with Apple, we use the email Apple provides only to suggest an initial handle — we never store it.
  • Match data you create: results, opponents, timestamps, and the location of a match you host. A match's venue coordinates are stored so other players can find and join it, and are shown as a pin on the discovery map.
  • Your device location, only when you opt in, to show matches near you and to place you on an opt-in local leaderboard. Your own last-known location stays on your device and is never uploaded. We never track location in the background.

We do not collect:

  • Real names (unless you put one in your display name)
  • Your email address
  • Contacts, calendar, or photos beyond ones you explicitly attach to a match
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Any analytics or tracking-SDK data

How we use it

Only to operate the app: show you matches, calculate your rating, and sync your data across your devices. We do not sell your data, we do not share it with advertisers, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Where it lives

Your data is stored on your device and synced through Apple's CloudKit. We do not run our own servers that hold your data.

A subset of fields (your handle, display name, opaque user ID, privacy flags, and reputation score) sync via CloudKit's public database so other players can find and play you. Bio, home city, avatar URL, last-seen time, and your last-known location stay strictly on your device.

Leaderboards

You start invisible. If you opt into a leaderboard, you can choose:

  • Anonymous — your rating and rough region are visible; your name and handle are not.
  • Identifiable — your handle and rating are visible.

You can switch back to “off” at any time, which removes you from all public leaderboards immediately. Leaderboards never reveal another player's location — rankings attach to where a match was played, not to where anyone is.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we send you alerts about your own activity — invitations, results awaiting your approval, referee duties, and rating changes. These are delivered through Apple's Push Notification service and contain only the minimum needed to describe the event.

Your rights

  • Export your data: Settings → Privacy → Export My Data delivers a full JSON archive of everything Forum Victorum holds about you.
  • Delete your account and all data: Settings → Account → Delete Account. This cascade-deletes your profile, ratings, matches you created, and rating events.
  • Under GDPR (EU/UK) and comparable laws, you can also email supportvictoribus@gmail.com to request a copy, correction, or deletion of your data.

Children

Forum Victorum is rated 12+. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email supportvictoribus@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes materially, this page and the in-app version (Settings → Privacy → Privacy Policy) will be updated and dated.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email supportvictoribus@gmail.com.
